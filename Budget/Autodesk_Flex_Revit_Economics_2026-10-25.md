# Autodesk Flex economics for Revit

Research date: 2026-10-25
Status: published US list prices, subject to change; no purchase authorized.

## Official published rates
- Revit: **10 Flex tokens per 24-hour usage period**.
- Standard tier (up to 4,999 tokens): **$3/token**, therefore **$30 per Revit usage day**.
- Autodesk announced a reduced minimum starting 2026-06-04: **33 tokens for $99** (previous minimum 100 tokens/$300).
- Minimum pack supports **three complete Revit usage days** (30 tokens) with 3 tokens remaining.
- Autodesk says tokens expire one year after purchase and do not roll over.
- Billing is triggered by opening/signing into the product, and reopening within the same 24-hour period does not charge another daily allotment. Continued use in later periods can incur additional charges.
- Taxes and separately metered cloud services may add cost.

## Project strategy
Use Revit 2027 trial for baseline BIM; validate Autodesk Education eligibility first; after trial, prepare changes and scripts offline, then batch Revit execution into deliberate paid days. Confirm current prices and purchase quantities before spending. No Flex purchase without owner approval.
