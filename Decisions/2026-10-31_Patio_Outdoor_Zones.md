# Patio outdoor zones — 2026-10-31

**Status:** owner-selected adjacency direction; dimensions and site placement remain open.

## Owner decisions

- Organize the patio as a dining zone and a separate fire/lounge zone, connected by a clear walking route.
- Reserve space for a future outdoor fireplace but defer its type, fuel, product and exact location.
- Reserve a clear route toward a future pool. The pool itself and its location remain unselected.
- Use the already-selected Adirondack furniture model when its exact source reference or asset is recovered.

## Design implications

Keep the house-to-porch-to-landing sequence connected to the patio circulation band. Arrange the dining and fire/lounge zones beside that band so movement toward the future pool does not pass through the fireplace seating area. This is an adjacency rule for later site fitting; it does not set patio size, orientation, grade, thresholds or route width.

- Adirondack model reference/source file.

This decision is not a code or accessibility determination and establishes no structural, professional, permit or construction-readiness approval.
