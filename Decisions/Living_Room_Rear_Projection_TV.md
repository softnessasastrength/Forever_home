# Living room television: large cabinet rear-projection TV

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: owner preference; model, size, and exact technology not selected.

## Clarification
Owner means a large, chunky, furniture-sized **rear-projection television** (classic 1990s/2000s cabinet TV), NOT a ceiling projector and motorized projection screen.

This supersedes the *equipment assumption* in Decisions/Living_Room_Projection_Concept.md. Do not treat the earlier 120–135-inch retractable screen proposal as approved.

## Architectural implications
- Reserve a dedicated media wall/alcove for a deep, heavy floor-standing TV cabinet, with measured width, height, depth, weight, viewing distance, ventilation, and service access determined from the chosen model.
- Coordinate with the separate rustic pellet stove hearth and keep TV away from stove heat plume and required clearances.
- Plan floor loading and practical delivery/replacement path through entries, turns, and stairs; verify against selected unit.
- Include DIRECTV compatibility (actual receiver outputs, HDMI/HDCP, component/composite where applicable, aspect ratio, resolution), potential external scaler/converter, audio connections, surge protection, and labeled cable routes.
- Compare CRT rear projection, DLP, LCD, and LCoS variants for picture quality, availability, lamp/part replacement, convergence/calibration, and power/heat.
- Keep accessible service panels, adequate cooling, and future-proof wiring.
