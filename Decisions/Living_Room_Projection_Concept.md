# Living room projection concept

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: owner preference for giant projection TV confirmed; size, projector technology, and placement are provisional.

## Intent
- Keep the rustic New England brick pellet-stove hearth as an architectural focal point.
- Include a giant projection-based TV/movie experience in the living room, ideally with the screen concealed when not in use.
- Initial screen study: retractable approximately 120–135 inch diagonal 16:9, subject to seating distance, room geometry, budget, ambient light, and manufacturer constraints.
- Prefer a separate projection wall instead of mounting screen above or in the heat plume of the pellet stove.
- Compare conventional ceiling-mounted long-throw projector with UST projector and compatible screen. Motorized retractable screen compatibility, flatness/tension, and UST/ALR requirements must be verified rather than assumed.
- Plan blackout/shade control, acoustic treatment integrated into traditional finishes, projector ventilation, power, conduit for HDMI/fiber and control, and receiver/DIRECTV equipment location.
- Keep local wired AV distribution, cable replacement access, service clearances, and low-noise HVAC/ventilation.
- No projector model, throw distance, screen size, lumen rating, or speaker layout approved yet.

## Plan studies
A. Hearth on one wall, retractable screen on adjacent/opposite wall, seating arranged to enjoy both.
B. Dedicated projection wall with screen concealed in ceiling trim or cabinetry.
C. Optional projector screen in front of shelving, if safe clearance and practical access allow.

## Technical checks
Sightlines and screen center height; projector throw and lens shift; 16:9 screen physical dimensions; seating distances; daylight control; ambient light performance; heat/soot separation; cable length and format; screen motor service; acoustics; cost and maintenance.
