# Critical-load battery backup with gas generator supplement

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: owner-selected backup strategy; engineering and equipment sizing pending.

## Owner decision
- Favor battery backup of **essential loads for multiple days** over powering the entire house normally during an outage.
- Allow a **gas-fueled generator** to supplement batteries for prolonged outages.
- Solar and batteries remain non-negotiable; retain the separate >=50% annual solar electricity target.
- Generator fuel type (gasoline, natural gas or propane), portable vs standby, and integration are not yet selected.

## Preliminary design
- Dedicated essential-load panel or equivalent controllable circuits for refrigeration, pellet heating controls and fans, network/communications, essential lighting and outlets, and water/sump pumps where relevant.
- Initially model 24–48 hours of autonomy without generator, then assess longer-duration scenarios and winter solar yield; this is a proposal, not an owner-approved fixed runtime.
- Model battery usable kWh, discharge kW, inverter surge capacity, standby loads, reserve state of charge and solar recharge during grid outage.
- Provide generator-ready transfer/isolation equipment, exterior generator placement and wiring route; ensure chosen inverter/battery system explicitly supports safe generator operation and charging.
- Consider discretionary AV loads (Mitsubishi CRT and Kenwood hi-fi) separately from essential loads.
- Generator transfer must prevent backfeeding the grid. Combustion generator must operate outdoors with code-compliant clearance from openings and air intakes, never in a garage or enclosed porch.
- Coordinate solar, battery, generator and utility switching through qualified electrical design, permitting and utility interconnection.

## Open questions
- Target minimum essential-load runtime without sun or generator.
- Fuel source and generator form factor.
- Whether a well pump, sump pump, or other site-specific loads are essential.
- Whether generator should auto-start at low battery or be manually started.
