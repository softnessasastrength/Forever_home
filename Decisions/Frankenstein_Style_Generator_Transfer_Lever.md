# Frankenstein-style manual generator transfer lever

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: owner-selected aesthetic; implementation to be engineered.

## Owner direction
Owner wants the generator transfer control to resemble the dramatic, oversized industrial knife/lever switches associated with old mad-scientist laboratories.

## Preferred implementation
Seek a **listed, suitably rated, enclosed manual transfer switch with a substantial external operating lever**. A visually theatrical, tactile mechanism is desirable, but all live switching contacts must remain guarded and inaccessible.

Alternative: a dramatic decorative/low-voltage lever interfaced only through a manufacturer-approved control scheme to listed transfer equipment. Never improvise a control modification that defeats listing, interlocking or safety protections.

## Safety and functional constraints
- Do not use an exposed antique knife switch for actual utility/generator transfer.
- Transfer mechanism must provide proper utility/generator isolation and prevent backfeed; installation, neutral switching/bonding, overcurrent protection and equipment ratings require licensed electrical design.
- Preserve automatic battery takeover of essential circuits during utility outage; the theatrical manual lever is for the **manually started generator** transfer process, not for routine battery switchover.
- Confirm inverter/charger and generator compatibility and an appropriate sequence before specifying a device.
- Mount at a safe, accessible, dry location with adequate working clearances; avoid exposing visitors or children to electrical hazards.

- Actual listed equipment model and lever geometry.
- Whether external handle can be visually integrated with an antique-style control cabinet without modifying safety certification.
- Mechanical manual transfer vs manufacturer-supported remote-control arrangement.
- Labeling, placement and the safe start/transfer/shutdown operating procedure.
