# Roofline concept — 2026-10-31

**Status:** owner-selected massing direction for further study; pitch, ridge and roof construction remain unselected.

## Owner direction

Use one dominant traditional main gable with a visually subordinate, simpler roof over the three-season porch. This follows the warm New England direction and avoids committing the main massing to intersecting cross-gables before the room and shell layout is coordinated.

## Not decided

- Roof pitch, eave/ridge elevations, ridge orientation, dormers, overhangs, valleys, porch-roof slope, and transitions remain unassigned.
- No framing, snow/wind load, drainage, flashing, energy or code conclusion is made.
- The tested 8:12, 10:12 and 12:12 cases in the upper-suite studies are geometric comparisons only. They are not owner selections or structural recommendations.
- Site, municipality, permit date and actual applicable code path remain unknown. Connecticut remains the research jurisdiction.

## Next study

After the main room layout and shell are coordinated, compare roof elevations and sections for the single-gable massing; carry a secondary cross-gable only if the plan benefits from it. A qualified architect/engineer must resolve roof geometry and structure for the actual site.
