# Owner design decisions: heating, future accessibility, porch

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: design intent approved; technical implementation subject to design and code review.

## Pellet-based heating
- Preserve pellet-based heating as a defining feature of Forever Home, including a visible living-room hearth/stove.
- Evaluate whether pellet equipment is supplemental or primary heat through an actual room-by-room heat-loss calculation, distribution plan, and cold-weather resilience analysis.
- Reserve appropriate space for safe pellet storage, delivery, ash handling, appliance maintenance, combustion air and listed vent routing.
- Provide a code-compliant backup heating strategy and evaluate electrical needs for augers, fans, controls, and power outages.
- Final stove location, chimney/vent clearances, fire protection, emissions and local approvals require qualified design and installation.

## Future accessibility
- Future accessibility is an explicit requirement, especially because the split-level concept creates multiple short stair runs.
- Develop an accessible route strategy between entry, primary daily-living spaces, a full bathroom, and a bedroom-capable space.
- Study feasible lift/elevator placement and reserve a coordinated shaft/landing footprint if appropriate; do not assume a stairlift alone solves every level transition.
- Plan adaptable door widths, turning spaces, bathroom reinforcement and clearances, and practical garage-to-home entry.
- Test the entire split-level section and route continuity before fixing stair locations. Assess a main-level bedroom/bath alternative if vertical access proves impractical.

## Porch
- Retain the three-season porch concept, approximately 12 ft x 10 ft as a preliminary program size.
- Access from dining via French doors as currently envisioned.
- Consider future conversion only if economical, but do not upgrade to a four-season room by default.
- Resolve insect screening, weather protection, floor drainage, structure/foundation, door threshold, and accessibility.
