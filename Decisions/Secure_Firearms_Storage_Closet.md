# Secure firearms storage closet

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: owner-required space; exact dimensions, safe and location TBD.

## Owner intent
Include a small, discreet closet or alcove for firearms storage. Safety, access control, and compliance with applicable storage laws are primary requirements.

## Preliminary spatial concept
- Reserve approximately 5 ft x 6 ft as a **conceptual placeholder**, not a finalized dimension.
- Use a separately lockable closet with a properly rated/constructed locking firearms safe inside, rather than open display racks.
- Provide secure unloaded firearm storage, with ammunition separately secured and access restricted to authorized users.
- Consider reliable access-control hardware and a documented key/code management plan; never record access codes or safe combinations in this repository.
- Keep location discreet and out of public/guest circulation. Avoid adjacency to pellet-stove heat and battery equipment.
- Include suitable lighting, dry conditioned environment and humidity monitoring to mitigate corrosion.
- Design safe anchorage per manufacturer guidance and verify concentrated floor/slab loads including fully loaded safe weight.
- Do not obstruct required egress, electrical equipment clearances, plumbing access or fire/life safety systems.

## Code and legal review
- Once jurisdiction is selected, verify current state/local firearms storage and child-access prevention requirements, including any rules for ammunition storage and transport.
- Confirm safe manufacturer's anchoring, installation and environmental requirements.
- Review structural capacity, applicable building/fire/electrical codes, and access/egress conditions with qualified professionals.
- Do not represent this concept as certified legal compliance before site, products and jurisdiction are known.

## Pending design decisions
- Number and size of firearms/safes, without storing identifying firearm details in public repository.
- Floor location and whether slab supported.
- Preferred closet vs recessed alcove layout and access-control scheme.
