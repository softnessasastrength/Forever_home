# Pantry-door comparison direction

Owner selected **Compare 30-in and wider door**. This authorizes a comparison, not a door selection.

Use the older kitchen specification's nominal 30-in door as the baseline and nominal 36 in as an explicitly proposed wider study case, derived from the existing 36-in circulation proxy. Nominal leaf/unit width is not actual clear opening. Preserve the 35-sf pantry, island and protected bathroom areas. Compare inward and outward swing where geometry supports a test; show route occupation while open. Door family/frame, actual clear width, shelving, hardware and applicable requirements remain unresolved.

A nominal 30-in opening is 6 in narrower than the 36-in route proxy; nominal 36 in only matches it arithmetically and supplies no allowance for frame, leaf, hardware or tolerance. Neither arithmetic result establishes access, code compliance or installation fit.
