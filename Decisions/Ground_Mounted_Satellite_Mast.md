# Ground-mounted satellite mast and architectural integration

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: owner preference, conditional on site survey and provider installation requirements.

## Owner direction
Prefer a **ground-mounted satellite dish mast** rather than a roof-mounted dish. Integrate the mast and equipment base into the site's New England architectural and landscape character. The structure should look intentional rather than added afterward.

## Conceptual treatment
- A discreet garden/utility composition, e.g. removable cedar lattice, low fieldstone-edged planting, or a modest split-rail/landscape setting.
- Conceal and protect the mast base and service entry where feasible, **not the dish reflector, feed arm, adjustment points, or satellite line of sight**.
- Avoid permanent masonry that traps or obstructs replacement of the mast, dish, or LNB.
- Maintain service access and alignment clearance; no trees/shrubs likely to grow into the required view corridor.

## Engineering requirements
- Site-specific line-of-sight assessment for DIRECTV **and** DISH, including seasonal vegetation and future growth; ground placement is not guaranteed feasible.
- Rigid engineered mast/foundation for local wind loads, frost depth, soil and snow exposure; protect against impacts from mowers and snow equipment.
- Underground conduit to accessible central satellite distribution bay, with cable-fill/bend-radius allowances, weather-rated RG-6 and spares, water management and replaceable cable pulls.
- Code-compliant grounding and bonding, surge considerations, separation from power utilities, and suitable exterior penetrations.
- Provider approval of antenna mounting arrangement, maximum cable runs and required electronics.
- Landscape screens must not affect dish alignment, ventilation, snow shedding or safe access.

## Design status
Ground mast is preferred, **not a final approved site position**. Position, orientation, mast height, foundation and enclosure dimensions remain TBD until lot survey and provider-specific antenna geometry.
