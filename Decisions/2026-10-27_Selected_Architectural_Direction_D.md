## Tradeoffs and limits

The lower-level flex room preserves main-floor space and the original lower-level adaptable-living concept. Access from daily living to that bedroom and the lower full bath remains stair-dependent unless a suitable lift or site-specific route is designed. The selected main half bath has no shower and does not by itself provide a step-free sleeping-and-full-bathing arrangement. The B shell is a larger test plate than A and should not be mistaken for an accepted area target. The half bath reduces the former proposal's fixture scope, but its actual fit and service route are not yet established.

Direction D is documented at [Selected Design Iteration 2026-10-27](../Architecture/Selected_Design_Iteration_2026-10-27/README.md). Its room-block study relocates lower technology outside the upper Jack-and-Jill footprint and makes a six-inch guest/bath adjustment to avoid projecting the upper bath over lower L-02. Those are schematic layout proposals subject to review, not approved construction geometry.
