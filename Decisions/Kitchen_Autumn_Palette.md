# Kitchen palette — restrained autumn tones

Status: owner-approved aesthetic direction; actual paints and finishes require physical samples in intended lighting.

- Overall feeling: modest, muted autumn; warm, traditional, cozy, not saturated or themed.
- Island cabinetry: subdued olive/sage with a brown/gray undertone (think dried sage or soft moss), avoiding bright mint and vivid green.
- Perimeter cabinets: warm ivory/cream, not stark white.
- Island countertop: approximately 6 ft warm cream quartz and 2 ft butcher block as recorded in Kitchen_Island_Finish.md.
- Wood: natural warm oak floors; honey/walnut-toned butcher block without orange stain.
- Metals: understated aged brass or antique bronze, not polished gold.
- Backsplash: warm ivory, biscuit, or muted handmade-look tile.
- Accent colors for textiles and small furnishings: faded terracotta, muted rust, oatmeal, dusty mustard, and deep brown in small doses.
- Lighting: warm residential lighting with careful color rendering; evaluate all samples in daylight and evening.
- Avoid pumpkin-orange cabinetry, high-contrast black-and-white, cool gray, and overly coordinated seasonal decor.

The aim is a kitchen that feels gently autumnal year-round, not decorated for October.
