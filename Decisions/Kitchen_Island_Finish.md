# Kitchen island finish decision

Status: owner-selected concept, pending shop drawings and material samples.

- Island target: 8 ft x 4 ft, subject to dimensioned clearance and support review.
- Countertop: approximately 6 ft warm cream quartz plus 2 ft real butcher block at one end, joined with a deliberate, visually clean transition.
- Range: 30 in induction unit on the perimeter wall, with appropriately designed ducted hood.
- Sink and dishwasher: perimeter, leaving the island free of plumbing and cooking appliances.
- Intended aesthetic: warm traditional / whimsical grandma; cream Shaker cabinets, aged brass, oak flooring.
- Design and fabrication to resolve: orientation of the 6/2 split, seam construction and differential movement, material thickness/flush transition, support and overhang, edge profile, sealing and cleaning, and seating clearances.
- The 6 ft/2 ft dimensions are a design intention, not approved fabrication measurements.

## Height coordination note — 2026-10-27

- The kitchen modeling reference specifies a 34-1/2 in base cabinet and a 1-1/4 in counter. Their sum is 35-3/4 in, which is 1/4 in below the same reference's nominal 36 in counter height.
- The owner accepted that 1/4 in arithmetic variance as reasonable for the nominal 36 in study target. Use the stated 34-1/2 in base and 1-1/4 in counter values; do not add a shim or silently alter the countertop thickness to force 36 in.
- This resolves the study's nominal-height interpretation only. Material thickness/flush transition, the 6 ft/2 ft finish orientation, seam, support, overhang and shop drawings remain open. A generic test mass represents no approved material or fabrication detail.
