# Concept direction — 2026-10-25

Status: owner-approved design intent; geometry and cost are NOT approved.

## Decisions
- The prior approximately 1,500 sf target was a back-of-envelope placeholder, **not a size cap**.
- Determine gross and conditioned area from the room program, circulation, structure, accessibility, and cost.
- Exterior: modest, welcoming New England traditional / raised-ranch-inspired street presence; the home opens into a surprisingly spacious interior.
- Explore a sloped lot and rear walkout lower level to conceal mass from the street; do not assume a site has been selected.
- Preserve the warm, collected, whimsical-grandma character and human-scale rooms, not an oversized undifferentiated open plan.
- Continue the connected bedroom suite, boutique, hybrid office, and generous bathrooms, subject to door and circulation review.
- Plan detailed low-voltage and media infrastructure, including an actual DIRECTV satellite system once equipment details are known.
- Design the BIM asset pipeline for an eventual interactive desktop walkthrough and VR experience.
