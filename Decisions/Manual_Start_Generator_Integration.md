# Manual-start generator integration

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: owner-selected operating mode; equipment engineering pending.

## Approved direction
- Generator will be **manually started by the homeowner**, not automatically started at low battery charge or grid failure.
- Solar and battery system remains mandatory, with a >=50% annual solar electricity contribution target.
- Batteries should **automatically maintain designated essential circuits** when the utility fails, using suitable islanding and transfer equipment.
- Generator is supplemental for extended outages and should be able to support essential loads and, if supported by the chosen inverter/charger, recharge batteries.

## Design requirements
- Code-compliant generator inlet or permanently installed generator connection and approved transfer/interlock equipment to prevent utility backfeed.
- Generator location outdoors, with compliant clearance from windows, doors, air intakes and occupied spaces; carbon monoxide safety provisions.
- Compatible inverter/charger generator-input specification, stable power-quality requirements, and load management; never assume arbitrary generator output is accepted by a hybrid inverter.
- Simple, documented manual connection, start, transfer, charging and shutdown sequence.
- Generator fuel type, portable vs stationary form factor, wattage, connection type, and storage remain open pending loads and site requirements.
- Separate ordinary automatic battery takeover from manual generator startup; do not imply both are manual.
