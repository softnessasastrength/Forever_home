# Professional design discipline and digital deliverable

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: owner-approved project direction

## Core principle
The digital Forever Home is a valid finished outcome even if the house is never physically built. This does **not** reduce the rigor expected of the design. Target the discipline, traceability and coordination appropriate to a real architectural project, including documentation that could be presented to an architect for review.

## Design phases and deliverables
1. **Programming:** room-by-room program, occupancy/use, area targets, adjacency matrix, original-vision preservation charter, priorities and constraints.
2. **Schematic design:** coordinated plans, elevations, sections, circulation, preliminary site/foundation assumptions, accessibility strategy and alternatives.
3. **Design development:** architectural assemblies, structural concepts, mechanical/plumbing/electrical and low-voltage coordination, product selections, equipment clearances, energy strategy and preliminary cost model.
4. **Construction-document-level development:** drawing index, plans, sections, details, schedules, outline/full specifications as appropriate, quantity takeoffs, bill of materials, code-analysis register and change history.
5. **Independent professional review readiness:** clearly distinguish verified facts, assumptions, conceptual engineering and issues requiring licensed architect/engineer review; prepare review questions and coordination package.
6. **Digital twin:** VR environment derived from coordinated BIM/design, with provenance for geometry and materials; do not let visualization silently diverge from documented design.

## Quality gates
- Maintain design decision log, revision history, open-issues and risk register.
- Track cross-discipline clashes, dimension conflicts, access/maintenance clearances and material/buildability assumptions.
- Record sources and applicable code editions only once jurisdiction and permit context are established.
- Treat safety-critical unresolved issues as blockers to declaring a design review complete.
- Keep owner approval mandatory before merging the design branch into `main`.

## Integrity of the original vision
Protect the warm, whimsical, lived-in New England character and the original co-created intent where feasible. Changes for safety, access, feasibility and comfort must be documented as conscious tradeoffs rather than quiet erasures.

## Boundaries
An assistant-authored package is **not** a stamped or permit-approved set of plans. Real-world construction requires site-specific information, professional design/calculations where required, authority review, permits and inspections. Do not mislabel conceptual design or simulated systems as certified engineering.

## Definition of success
A fully coordinated, explorable, carefully documented digital home is a complete project outcome in its own right. Physical construction remains optional.
