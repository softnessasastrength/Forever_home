# Vision-preservation charter

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: proposed synthesis of approved intent; new tradeoffs require owner review.

## Purpose and acceptance
Preserve Branden and Renn's co-created home while developing coordinated architecture and an explorable digital twin. Physical construction is optional. This charter is ready for schematic use when every proposed adaptation identifies its reason, effect on daily life, and affected source documents. It does not approve geometry or certify engineering.

## Preserve
- Warm, collected, whimsical-grandma character with modest New England traditional street presence; human-scale rooms, books, textiles, plants and meaningful possessions.
- Togetherness with privacy: connected private rooms, a shared primary bedroom, Renn's boutique, and Branden's hybrid bedroom/office. Personal rooms have equal design priority.
- Comfortable conversation between kitchen, dining and living; a dining-connected three-season porch; welcoming guest accommodation.
- Rustic pellet hearth and independent whole-house heat; visible cabinet rear-projection TV character and serviceable traditional component hi-fi.
- Future accessibility, maintainable systems, and replacement routes from the start.
- Required solar and battery systems, essential-load resilience, manual generator operation, and adaptable low-voltage infrastructure.
- A digital home whose geometry, equipment records, costs and visualizations remain traceable to documented design.

## Allowable studies, not automatic approvals
Area, footprint, level offsets, door arrangements, assemblies, brands and exact fixture layouts may evolve to resolve access, structure, safety, cost or maintenance. The prior 1,500 sf placeholder is not a cap. Preliminary room sizes and total-area allowances are not approved construction dimensions.

Compare each material change using: original intent; proposed adaptation; trigger/evidence; daily-life effect; area/cost/access implications; affected documents; owner decision and date. Do not quietly remove either personal room, eliminate the porch, replace the hearth character, or introduce a new aesthetic to simplify modeling.

## Before freezing schematic geometry
1. Reconcile room IDs, area accounting and independent circulation.
2. Draw all levels together in plan and section, including structure, stairs and a continuous future-accessible route.
3. Test kitchen work aisles and dining chair clearance using actual envelopes.
4. Test bath privacy, guest access, bedroom-capable spaces and equipment delivery/service routes.
5. Record alternatives and unresolved issues in the coordination register.
