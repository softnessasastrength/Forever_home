# Service intervals

Proposed planning checklist; manufacturer instructions and local requirements override generic intervals. Establish actual dates at commissioning.

| Frequency | Review |
|---|---|
| Monthly | Check visible leaks, alarms per manufacturer, HVAC filter condition |
| Seasonally | Review gutters, drainage, exterior seals, freeze protection, HVAC readiness |
| Annually | Arrange appropriate HVAC and pellet stove service; inspect roof/envelope safely; review insurance and asset register |
| After major weather or repairs | Inspect affected assemblies and update records |

Record asset ID, date, task, technician, findings, parts, cost, evidence, next due date and unresolved issues. Use qualified trades for hazardous work.
