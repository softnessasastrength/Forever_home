# Construction lifecycle plan

Status: proposed documentation framework, not construction authorization. No final area or schematic is approved. Licensed professionals and local authorities govern engineering, permits and inspections.

| Phase | Required evidence before release |
|---|---|
| 00 Site and design | Survey, zoning, utilities, geotechnical needs, selected schematic, accessibility review, approved budget and permit drawings |
| 01 Foundation | Excavation and drainage plans, engineered foundation, inspection records, concrete tickets, waterproofing photographs before backfill |
| 02 Structure | Approved framing drawings, load paths, connector schedule, framing inspections, documented deviations |
| 03 Envelope | Roofing, flashing, window installation, air/water barrier details, concealed-work photos, moisture checks |
| 04 MEP | Coordinated trade drawings, circuit and valve schedules, rough-in inspections, pressure and electrical tests by qualified trades |
| 05 Interiors | Finish samples, approved material codes, waterproofing inspections, installation records, punch list |
| 06 Commissioning | Final inspections, occupancy authorization, equipment commissioning, as-built drawings, warranties and owner training |

For every phase record scope, dependencies, responsible party, drawing revision, quantities, actual cost, dates, inspection authority, acceptance evidence, open defects and sign-off. Do not conceal work before required inspections. Record changes before implementation; stop and escalate safety or structural discrepancies.

## Daily log template

Date / phase / weather / crew / work completed / materials received / photos (location and orientation) / tests / incidents / deviations / next actions. Store sensitive site and security details privately.
