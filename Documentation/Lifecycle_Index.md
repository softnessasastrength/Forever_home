## Document control

Use stable IDs for rooms, assemblies, items, assets and decisions. Every controlled document records status (draft/proposed/approved/superseded/as-built), revision, date, owner, source and related IDs. Link rather than duplicate authoritative specifications. Preserve superseded revisions in Git. Require owner review for design changes and professional approval where applicable. Reconcile as-built records against actual installations before closing a phase.

## Additional registers to develop

Site due diligence, permit and inspection register, risk register, budget and change orders, accessibility verification, energy modeling, commissioning, concealed-work photo index, warranty and handover package. Track these as open work until evidence exists.

## Public repository boundary

Do not commit exact future address, security layouts, access credentials, personal financial records, unredacted invoices or sensitive serial numbers. Use private references for restricted records. Review photos and metadata before publication.
