## Adjacency and privacy brief
| Pair / route | Intent | Test before approval |
|---|---|---|
| Primary ↔ boutique ↔ hybrid | Private internal connections | Which pairs connect directly; doors, acoustic separation and furnished wall space |
| Primary → primary bath | Exclusive access | No guest or through circulation |
| Hybrid ↔ Jack & Jill ↔ guest | Shared bathroom | Lock coordination, emergency release, door conflicts; bath not required circulation |
| Guest → shared circulation | Independent access proposed | Avoid crossing primary, boutique or occupied bath |
| Kitchen ↔ dining ↔ porch | Open social space and direct French doors | Dining chairs, work aisles, thresholds and outdoor weather protection |
| Kitchen ↔ living | Open/wide connection; half-wall study | Furnishing, sightlines and circulation without sacrificing cozy proportions |
| Garage → arrival → kitchen | Practical protected groceries route | Stairs, weather boundary and future accessibility |
| Entry → daily living → full bath → sleeping space | Continuous future-accessible route required | Compare reserved lift/shaft with main-level bedroom/bath alternative |
| Hearth ↔ TV / hi-fi | Separate coordinated focal areas | Listed heat clearances, ventilation, service and floor loads |

## Kitchen baseline
Follow [island finish](../Decisions/Kitchen_Island_Finish.md) and [autumn palette](../Decisions/Kitchen_Autumn_Palette.md): approximately 8 x 4 ft appliance-free island, approximately 6 ft cream quartz plus 2 ft butcher block; muted olive/sage island, warm ivory perimeter cabinets, oak and aged brass. The 30 in induction range and ducted hood are on the perimeter. Older island-range descriptions are superseded.

## Open schematic decisions
- Coordinate footprint, floor elevations, structure and stair geometry; do not freeze historical six-riser levels without a complete section.
- Resolve direct suite-door pairs and guest boundary; check actual furniture and bedroom requirements.
- Decide main-floor public bathroom provision and continuous accessible sleeping/bathing route.
- Validate kitchen footprint against cabinet depths, island orientation, seating and 42–48 in study aisles.
- Verify actual TV and stove dimensions/manuals before sizing built-ins, hearth or alcove.
- Establish site, jurisdiction, grade, solar exposure, driveway and walkout feasibility.

See [vision charter](../Project/Vision_Preservation_Charter.md) and [coordination register](../Project/Coordination_Register.md). No wall layout, area total or code compliance is approved by this program.
