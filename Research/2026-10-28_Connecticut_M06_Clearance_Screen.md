## Adopted-code context

Connecticut DAS identifies the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code as currently applicable to permits filed from October 1, 2022. It adopts the 2021 International Residential Code (IRC), 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), and references ICC A117.1-2017. The 2026 code cycle had been expected to take effect July 1, 2026, but DAS reports its effective date is delayed pending Legislative Regulation Review Committee approval. Recheck the official state status against the actual permit date and local building official before relying on a code edition.

For a detached one-family dwelling that falls within IRC scope, IRC § R307.1 directs fixture spacing to Figure R307.1 and § P2705.1. The 2021 IRC § P2705.1 general fixture-installation text specifies for water closets, lavatories and bidets: at least 15 in from the fixture center to a side wall, partition or vanity; at least 30 in center-to-center between adjacent fixtures; and at least 21 in clear in front to a wall, fixture or door. The 2021 IRC Figure R307.1 also governs fixture spacing. Treat these as preliminary minimum geometric screening constraints for the WC and lavatory, subject to confirming the adopted local text, amendments, measurement datums and exact applicability.

## Implications for the M-06 search

- Keep B unassigned. For any candidate product, measure its actual bowl, tank, seat and mounting extents; check the product manufacturer's installation envelope separately from the code screening dimensions.
- Include the WC centerline, adjacent side obstacles, lavatory envelope, front clearance and door leaf/swing in the same dimensioned plan. A route sample or nominal room block is not this check.
- Show the approach from the public hall, privacy at the entry, and a plausible sanitary/water/vent route before considering a placement supported. These are separate design coordination checks, not established by § R307.1.
- Do not label this a wheelchair-accessible design. Applicable accessibility scoping depends on project/occupancy and the adopted code; accessibility standards can require substantially different clearances. Owner preferences for usability do not by themselves establish legal applicability.
- The 15/30/21 in screening values do not define the room size, establish comfortable use, resolve door maneuvering, service routing or construction detail, or establish code compliance. A licensed design professional and the authority having jurisdiction must confirm project-specific requirements.
