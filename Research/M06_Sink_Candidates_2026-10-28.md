A pedestal and a vanity are different design choices: the pedestal preserves a lighter open floor appearance but offers no enclosed storage; a vanity may add storage but needs a specific product and a verified depth/door/approach arrangement. Do not assign either form from style alone.

## Gross-envelope screen, and what it does not establish

Selected-D search window B is documented as a 13 ft 2 in × 7 ft gross room-block gap (not clear interior dimensions). The Evolution combo 22 × 17-1/2 in, Tresham 24 × 19-1/2 in and Edgemere 25 × 19-1/2 in body rectangles are each smaller than B in either plan orientation. `Tools/screen_m06_sink_candidates_gross_body.ps1` reproduces this source-based comparison and writes `M06_Sink_Gross_Body_Screen_2026-10-28.json`; the report records the selected-D input fingerprint and explicitly leaves every product, room and fit unassigned. This is only a body-rectangle comparison: it does not add the toilet, user approach, sink use zone, wall finish/build-up, door/swing, rough-in or service route. The fact that any sink rectangle can be drawn somewhere inside B does **not** demonstrate a half-bath layout or assign a room.

## Gate before any M-06 model placement

1. Decide whether the design needs a pedestal sink's open/lighter appearance or a storage vanity, using the documented house style and owner's priorities.
2. For the chosen live product, obtain the exact Revit family or author a visibly labelled schematic body envelope from the current manufacturer dimension sheet. Revit family dimensions must be measured from positive-volume solids; verify SKU, type, hosting/orientation and full basin/leg assembly. For Evolution 0467400.020, confirm the hosted `0467.400` solids actually show the pedestal despite the “Basin only” type description.
3. Resolve the selected toilet's exact overall model geometry and a cited half-bath-specific clearance basis for the known project jurisdiction/site. Then test toilet + lavatory, user approach, entry/door and a plausible service path in B without treating B's gross gap as an interior room.
4. Keep M-06 unassigned and preserve the owner-selected D space/IFC baseline until those inputs pass. No M-06 IFC should be exported from rectangles alone.

No code-compliance, accessibility, plumbing-feasibility, purchase or product-availability guarantee is made here. Confirm current price, stock, color and downloadable file at the point of selection.
