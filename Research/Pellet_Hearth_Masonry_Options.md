# Pellet hearth masonry options — preliminary investigation

Status: research and recommendations only. Owner has not selected masonry.

## Context
Rustic traditional pellet stove in living room; independent whole-house heating backup. Modest, muted autumn aesthetic.

## Aesthetic options
1. Weathered red clay brick with buff mortar: strongest timeless/rustic option.
2. Lightly limewashed red brick: softer and integrates with warm ivory / sage palette; avoid opaque chalky white.
3. New England fieldstone: regionally expressive but visually heavy; consider limited hearth/surround.
4. Honed warm limestone: understated and elegant, less rustic; evaluate porousness and hearth suitability.
5. Reclaimed-look brick wall in running bond with herringbone hearth: preferred detail exploration.

## Assemblies to compare
- Full-depth clay brick masonry: structural support, weight, footprint and cost implications.
- Real fired-clay thin-brick adhered veneer: appropriate substrate and mortar/attachment details; not equivalent to full structural masonry.
- Natural thin stone veneer: varied weight and setting methods; qualified detailing.
- Manufactured stone veneer: appearance/quality and approved installation.
- Solid stone or masonry hearth slab: specific stone and floor-protection requirements to verify.

## Key engineering distinctions
- Decorative masonry surround is **not** the same as a masonry chimney.
- Modern pellet appliances commonly use model-specific listed pellet vent; manufacturer may allow vent through an existing masonry chimney with approved liner/connection.
- Do not apply masonry fireplace hearth-extension dimensions to a freestanding pellet stove without checking the actual listed appliance manual.
- Noncombustible decorative veneer does not automatically reduce combustible-framing or mantel clearances.
- Validate floor protection, combustible clearances, vent routing, outside-air provisions, ash/maintenance access, hearth loading, and local approvals for the chosen stove.

## Recommendation to test
Weathered warm red/brown thin clay brick or appropriately supported full brick; restrained partial limewash; buff mortar; simple oak mantel subject to listed clearance; herringbone brick hearth. Compare physical samples and total installed costs.
