# Connecticut residential fixture clearance basis for M-06

Checked: **2026-11-01**
Status: **primary-source code research for schematic screening; not a project code review or compliance determination**

## Adoption and scope

Connecticut DAS currently identifies the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code (CSBC), based on the 2021 International Codes, as the code applying to permit applications filed on or after 2022-10-01. The CSBC's introductory provisions identify the 2021 International Residential Code (IRC) as the path for detached one- and two-family dwellings within its scope. The Connecticut amendment volume reviewed here lists amendments to that IRC but contains no amendment entry for IRC R307.1 or P2705.1. A project-specific code path still depends on the eventual site, building classification, permit timing, and authority having jurisdiction.

## Residential fixture provisions for a conservative plan screen

The 2021 IRC R307.1 directs fixture spacing to Figure R307.1 and Section P2705.1. The corresponding P2705.1 provisions state, for water closets and lavatories:

- Keep each water-closet or lavatory center at least **15 in** from any side wall, partition, or vanity.
- Keep adjacent fixture centers at least **30 in** apart.
- Provide at least **21 in** in front of a water closet or lavatory to a wall, fixture, or door.
- Locate piping, fixtures, and equipment so they do not interfere with operation of windows or doors.

For schematic M-06 work, measure these clearances from the actual fixture centerlines and finished obstructions once geometry is available. Check the full door swing and approach separately; the fixture rule's front-clearance dimension alone does not establish a usable room entry, accessibility, or a complete path. The adopted text also says plumbing fixtures must be usable. This note does not convert those provisions into an accessibility design standard.

## Application to M-06

Treat the dimensions above as a **screening basis only**, not as an assertion that the project is subject to a particular code edition or passes it. Current documented product evidence remains incomplete: the selected American Standard 610AA001.020 toilet has a plan-envelope proxy but no verified exact-SKU Revit family, and the KOHLER Tresham K-2844-4 is an inspected study candidate, not a selected lavatory. Its measured family bounds and sanitary connector are useful inputs but do not establish installed orientation, exact rough-in, finished clearances, or a service route.

Accordingly, do not assign M-06, A, or B from this code note. A later fit study still needs a product-geometry source appropriate to each selected fixture, finished wall/partition faces, a door location and operating envelope, arrival/approach geometry, and a plausible supply/drain/vent route. Keep west A protected per the owner's decision; keep B unassigned. Any proposed placement must remain a clearly labeled comparison in a disposable derivative until the evidence supports a design choice.
