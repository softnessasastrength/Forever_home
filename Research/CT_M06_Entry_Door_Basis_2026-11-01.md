# Connecticut residential entry-door basis for M-06

**Date:** 2026-11-01
**Status:** scoped code-text research; no door size, swing, room, or compliance determination selected.

## Finding

Connecticut's published 2022 State Building Code is based on the 2021 International Residential Code for qualifying one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses. Under 2021 IRC R311.2, each dwelling unit must have at least one egress door; that egress door must be side-hinged with at least 32 in clear width and 78 in clear height. The section explicitly says other doors are not required to meet those particular minimum dimensions.

For the M-06 study, this provision therefore does **not** establish a 32-in clear-width requirement for an ordinary interior powder-room door. It also does not establish a minimum width for that door from all other potentially applicable provisions. No door width is assigned by this note. If the powder-room door forms part of a route or serves a space subject to other requirements, that needs separate analysis against the project's confirmed code path and intended access goals.
