# M238 — Connecticut residential code status screen

**Research date:** 2026-10-31  
**Status:** authoritative jurisdictional research; not a code-compliance review.

## Findings

- Connecticut DAS's [current building-code resources](https://portal.ct.gov/das/services-for-agencies-and-municipalities/safety-codes-and-inspections) still identify the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code (CSBC) as current. DAS says it is based on the 2021 I-Codes, references ICC A117.1-2017, and applies to permit applications filed from 2022-10-01.
- DAS's [code-adoption status page](https://portal.ct.gov/das/office-of-state-building-inspector/building-and-fire-code-adoption-process) says the next codes were expected to take effect 2026-07-01, but the effective date has been delayed while approval by the Legislative Regulation Review Committee is pending. It lists 2024 model codes as anticipated bases for the 2026 codes, not as currently effective Connecticut requirements. No revised effective date is stated there.
- The official [2022 CSBC PDF](https://portal.ct.gov/das/-/media/das/office-of-state-building-inspector/2022-state-codes/2022-csbc-final.pdf?rev=a59cc11db0564caaa5477bd696ee85f2) identifies the 2021 IRC portion as applying to detached one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses no more than three stories above grade with a separate means of egress (R101.2, PDF p. 119). Final project classification, height and permit date are not established here.
- The same amended IRC explicitly gives a 36-in minimum for the unobstructed yard path to a public way in the stated emergency-escape exception (R310.1, PDF p. 137), and a 36-in clear stair width (R311.7.1, PDF p. 138). Those are section-specific values. They do **not**, by themselves, establish a universal 36-in interior hallway rule or a 36-in clear route through this house.

## Design consequence

Keep M215's 36-in route as a labeled analysis proxy, not a compliance claim. Before applying any code screen to M-06, kitchen circulation, doors, stairs, porch or future pool access, establish the project's jurisdiction, permit-application timing, code scope/classification and actual assemblies; then verify the applicable 2021 model provisions together with Connecticut amendments. The official DAS copy is the controlling starting source. A local building official or licensed design professional must resolve project-specific interpretation.

No room layout, dimension, fixture assignment, door, stair or structural decision is changed by this research. It does not establish code compliance, accessibility, structural safety, professional certification, permit approval or construction readiness.
