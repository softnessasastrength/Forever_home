Checked: **2026-10-30**

## Statewide code status

Connecticut DAS currently identifies the **2022 Connecticut State Building Code (CSBC)** as applying to permit applications filed on or after October 1, 2022. DAS says the planned 2026 code effective date has been delayed pending Legislative Regulation Review Committee approval. The 2022 CSBC adopts the 2021 International Residential Code (IRC), among other model codes. This is a statewide published-status check; the project town, permit filing date, building classification and local interpretation are not known.

## Clearance used for early layout screening

For a preliminary one-family residential screen, 2021 IRC Section R307.1 directs fixture spacing to Figure R307.1 and Section P2705.1. The provisions use a **15 in minimum from the water-closet center to a side wall, partition or vanity**, **30 in minimum center-to-center between adjacent fixtures**, and a **21 in minimum clear space in front** of a water closet, lavatory or bidet to a wall, fixture or door. P2705.1 also says fixture/piping location must not interfere with door or window operation. These are minimum fixture-spacing checks only. They do not establish an accessible bathroom, door clear width or maneuvering clearance, fixture usability, privacy, or a complete room fit.

The project has not established the municipality, applicable permit/code path, accessibility scope, finished wall faces, actual fixture location/datum, or door leaf/swing. Accordingly, the 15 in / 30 in / 21 in values may be used only to screen disposable layout proposals; they cannot be used to assign room B or claim code compliance. Before any assignment, the fit still needs manufacturer-confirmed toilet and lavatory geometry, finished-face clearances, an actual door and approach, and a plausible service route. A building official or qualified design professional must confirm project-specific applicability.
