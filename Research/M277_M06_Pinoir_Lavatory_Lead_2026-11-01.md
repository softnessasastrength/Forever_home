## Product lead

KOHLER lists the Pinoir K-2035-1 as a 22-in oval wall-mount lavatory with a concealed shroud. Its current product page describes ridged edges and subtle curves; this is a wall-mounted basin plus shroud, not a vanity and not the Tresham pedestal product. The manufacturer's current spec gives nominal overall dimensions of 22 × 18 in and a 34-in installed rim-height configuration. The bowl area is listed as 18 × 12 in. KOHLER's specification states it complies with ADA when installed to the specified requirements; that is a manufacturer statement and is not a Connecticut project determination.
