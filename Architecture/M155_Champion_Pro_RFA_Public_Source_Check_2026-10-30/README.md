## Finding and disposition

No exact-current-SKU RFA, public download, or current-SKU installation drawing was found in this search. The 610AA plan-size source remains the manufacturer's current infographic already recorded in the M120 inputs (19 × 30.25 in), not a verified full product-family envelope. The 2011 RevitCity entry is not fit evidence without its file, dimensions, source lineage and current model identity.

Do not combine predecessor RFAs and label the result as the current 610AA assembly. Continue to keep both M06-B and M06-H unassigned until current product geometry, finished-face clearances, a real door/approach and a plausible water/waste/vent route can be checked together.
